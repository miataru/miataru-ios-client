
## Description
```text
Keep control of how you share your location with miataru. Choose the Miataru server you want to use, add trusted devices, and decide which sharing and tracking features fit your needs.

See multiple devices on interactive maps, check distance and available details such as battery, altitude, speed, and place, and organize devices into groups. Add devices with a QR code, link, or manual entry. Plan routes with distance, estimated time, arrival time, and turn guidance. Switch to a high-contrast, full-screen navigation view with optional mirroring.

Choose foreground or background location sharing, with battery-conscious or more frequent location updates. Where supported by your server, restrict access to approved devices. You can also review recent location requests and choose whether to receive alerts for unknown visitors.

Use widgets for selected devices and ask Siri or Shortcuts for a device's last known location or a route. miataru is designed for iPhone and iPad, with a dedicated iPad layout. Location sharing depends on your server, device settings, permissions, and network availability.
```

## What's New
```text
Background location sharing resumes more reliably after interruptions. After restarting your device, your tracking settings are restored when you first unlock it. During trips, Smart tracking now works more efficiently while keeping the same detail and frequency of your location reports. Location updates are kept during temporary connection problems and sent later. When several locations arrive at once, they are sent in order so the newest position stays current.

The map and full-screen navigation now show the speed that matches the direction of your route.
```
